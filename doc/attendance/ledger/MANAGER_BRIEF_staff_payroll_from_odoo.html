Road to Staff Payroll from Odoo

Flow, progress and decisions · Prepared by M Jawad · 5 Nov 2026

Purpose

Run staff payroll from Odoo attendance instead of the BioTime report. Today HR exports BioTime and prepares payroll by hand because Odoo attendance cannot be trusted: one employee can have several rows for the same day, some punches never reach Odoo, gate and mobile records overwrite each other, and leave or job-mission days are sometimes stored as absent.

Goal: one trusted attendance record per employee per day, built from every source, that payroll can read directly.

The new flow

Resolver priority: approved leave → job mission (full) → holiday / weekly off → half-day JM → punches (gate + mobile) → absent.
